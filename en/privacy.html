Light and dark themes

When you change the theme, your browser saves a preference named df-theme in local storage on your device. This restores your choice on your next visit. The preference stays there until you clear the site data or your browser removes it. The site serves fonts from its own domain and uses no advertising or profiling tools.

Optional analytics

Withdrawal stops future measurement and removes analytics cookies, without affecting earlier lawful processing. Google may process data outside the EEA.
